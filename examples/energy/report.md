In 2025 renewables provided 16.9% of Israel's electricity, up from 1.9% in 2015. Coal fell from 45.4% of generation in 2015 to 3.4% in 2025. Israel remains behind its Mediterranean peers: in 2025 renewables provided 55.9% of electricity in Spain and 48.8% in Italy. Total generation rose from 64.2 TWh in 2015 to 82.1 TWh in 2025, an increase of about 28%. Gas supplied 79.7% of Israel's electricity in 2025, so the main risk for the coming years is dependence on a single fuel.
